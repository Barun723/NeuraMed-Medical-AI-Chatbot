The Pipeline

How NeuraMed
Thinks & Answers

A transparent look at the 4-step neural pipeline from your question to a precise, evidence-backed answer.

01

You Ask a Question

Type any medical question in plain English — symptoms, conditions, medications, treatments. NeuraMed understands natural language just like a doctor would.

02

Semantic Vectorization

HuggingFace sentence-transformers encode your query into a 768-dimensional vector, capturing true semantic meaning well beyond keyword matching.

03

FAISS Vector Retrieval

FAISS performs lightning-fast approximate nearest-neighbor search across the entire indexed medical corpus, returning the most relevant evidence passages.

04

Groq LLM Synthesis

LangChain combines your question with the retrieved context and sends it to Groq's LPU. The LLM synthesizes a precise, readable, grounded answer in milliseconds.
